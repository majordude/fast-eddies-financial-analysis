HOW IT WORKS - Fast Eddies Financial Spending and Income Analysis

PRIVACY
  The redactor (presidio_redact_statements.py) reads the original PDFs in Inputs and writes redacted copies to
  Redacted. It removes names, SSNs, bank and account numbers, addresses and e-mail addresses on header, summary and
  address lines, and keeps the last four digits of card and account numbers. Transaction rows (lines that start with a
  date) are left alone so vendors and amounts stay readable. After saving each file the redactor reopens it and checks
  that no e-mail address or holder name remains; if one does, it deletes the file and prints LEAK CHECK FAILED.
  It is automatic, so spot check a file or two. It can blank part of a merchant name; the analyzer labels those
  "MERCHANT NAME NOT LEGIBLE" and the Review tab counts them.
  The analyzer (analyze_statements.py) reads only the redacted copies.

HOW THE NUMBERS ARE CHECKED
  Credit cards: each statement is compared with its own account summary (previous balance, payments, credits,
  purchases, fees, interest, new balance).
  Bank: for every account and month, opening balance plus all rows equals the closing balance, the running balance
  holds row by row, and deposits and withdrawals match the statement's summary table.
  Transfers tab: opening balance + income - expenses + net transfers equals the statement closing balance, every
  month. The difference row must be zero.
  Anything that does not tie is highlighted and listed on the Review tab. Nothing is silently dropped; an unreadable
  gap becomes its own line labelled "UNRECONCILED - REVIEW".

DECISIONS BUILT IN
  A statement counts in the month it closes (cash month = statement month).
  Card payments from the bank are transfers once the card's own statement shows the same payment (same amount,
  within a few days). Card spend comes from the card statements, so nothing is counted twice. A bank payment to a
  card with no matching statement payment stays an expense and is listed on the Review tab.
  Payments to cards whose statements are not loaded yet are an expense line ("Credit card payments - other cards"),
  flagged until those statements are added.
  Card interest and fees charged on the card statements are an expense line ("Card interest & fees"); they are paid
  through the card payments, which are transfers.
  Apple Card: all charges, returns and dispute credits are tied to the statement's own totals and balances. Daily
  Cash is paid into Apple Cash, not the card balance, so it is a memo (Apple Card tab, Reconciliation, Transactions
  column R) and is never netted from spend. A return also takes back the Daily Cash earned on it; that amount is part
  of the statement's charges and is folded into the return line.
  Synchrony store cards (Amazon Prime Store Card): the page-1 summary and the Account Balance Summary table are both
  read and must agree; purchases, credits, payments, fees and interest are tied to those totals and to previous
  balance - payments & credits + purchases + interest = new balance. Every purchase keeps its item name (Store Card
  Items tab). A payment on a statement pays the previous statement, so a bank payment becomes a transfer only when that
  previous statement is loaded; otherwise it stays an expense and nothing is lost or counted twice.
  Brokerage transfers into the bank count as income under their own label, with a separate row showing net cash flow
  before them. Money sent back to the brokerage is a transfer.
  Transfers between the bank's own accounts must net to zero.
  Checks show only a number on the statement, so they appear as "Checks written (payee not on statement)" unless
  listed in check_payees.json.
  A manual spend list marked as a reference account is a cross-check only; the bank statements are the source.
  Each calendar year gets its own workbook. A year with statements only through, say, September is named
  Income_vs_Expense_2026_Jan-Sep.xlsx and titled "year to date through Sep".

WORKBOOK TABS
  Advisor Summary, Advisor Categories   income, transfers and spending by month for the year, spending grouped;
                                        also saved on its own (values only, no transactions)
  Cash Flow                             income by source and expenses by category, by month, with net cash flow
  Income vs Expense Charts              income by source, income vs expenses, expense categories, brokerage in/out
  Transfers                             money moved, brokerage cross-check, balance tie-out
  Grand Totals, Charts, one tab per card    card spend, Pareto drivers, payments, interest, fraud and breach flags
  Transactions, Bank Transactions       every row, filterable, with category, source file and original description
  Reconciliation, Bank Reconciliation   proof for every statement
  Processed Files, Review               what was read, and everything that needs a human look

ADDING ANOTHER BANK OR CARD ISSUER
  The bank parser reads one credit-union layout by position: section headers, the date, description, amount and
  balance columns, and the summary table. A different bank layout needs its own parser that returns the same
  structure (accounts, rows, opening and closing balances, summary figures); classification, charts and
  reconciliation then work unchanged. A new card issuer needs its own statement reader (see parse_apple_statement
  and build_apple_rows, or parse_synchrony_statement for a card with item names) and an entry in CARD_LINKS.
